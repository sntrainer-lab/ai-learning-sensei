# AI Learning Session

Turn a chosen AI topic into demonstrated capability. Coordinate with the repository's `ai-learning-sensei` session lifecycle; this skill governs the teaching mechanics inside the practice phase.

## Establish the learning target

Before teaching, state a concrete capability and observable success criterion. Ask at most one short diagnostic question or tiny task when the learner's starting point is unclear. Do not repeat concepts they already demonstrate.

## Use the active-learning loop

Repeat the smallest useful version of this loop:

1. **Orient:** explain the mechanism in plain language and connect it to the learner's goal.
2. **Predict or recall:** ask the learner to anticipate an outcome, explain a choice, or retrieve a relevant idea before showing the answer when this will improve understanding.
3. **Practise:** give one to three concrete actions on a real or realistic task.
4. **Inspect:** wait for evidence, examine the result, and identify the highest-value correction.
5. **Improve:** let the learner revise, compare, or run a second attempt.
6. **Transfer:** ask how the method changes a nearby work task or give one small variant that requires choosing rather than copying.

Keep explanations proportional to the next action. Do not dump a full tutorial, hide unexplained commands in a checklist, or mistake tool setup for learning.

Before every practical batch retain the parent protocol headings:

- `Что сейчас:` the mechanism being practised;
- `Зачем тебе:` its connection to the learner's goal;
- `Сделай:` one to three actions.

## Give useful feedback

Base feedback on the artifact, answer, execution trace, or learner explanation. Separate:

- what works and should be retained;
- the single most important gap or misconception;
- the next revision and how success will be checked.

Do not complete the substantive practice on the learner's behalf unless they are blocked and need a worked example. When showing an example, follow it with a changed task that the learner performs.

## Verify learning before closure

Before the parent skill closes the session, obtain compact evidence of learning through one suitable check:

- a teach-back in the learner's own words;
- a choice between alternatives with justification;
- a correction of a flawed example;
- a small transfer task;
- reconstruction of the workflow without copying.

Record what the learner can now do, not merely what was covered. If the check exposes a gap, label it for later reinforcement rather than claiming mastery.
