# AI learning policy

## Relevance gate

Every active item must teach an AI capability: models, prompting, agents, AI automation, RAG and knowledge systems, AI-assisted text/visual/data workflows, evaluation, observability, safety, or AI product implementation.

Reject pure industry news, conventional software with no AI mechanism, vague hype, events, pricing announcements, and passive content with no plausible experiment.

## Personal priority model

Use `learning_goals`, `real_work_tasks`, `priority_areas`, `low_priority_areas`, current level, and recent ratings. Favor direct goal fit, near-term use, reuse across tasks, novelty, an observable result within the agreed duration, and credible evidence.

Source popularity and freshness are signals, not substitutes for relevance. A manual item still must pass the AI gate. After usefulness 1–2, down-rank adjacent setup-only topics. After difficulty 5, prefer a lighter applied win unless the learner asks to continue.

Run `sensei_store.py maintain`; it overwrites the configured backlog file. Keep roughly 15–30 active items. Each row needs a stable ID, outcome-oriented capability, concrete session result, and verified source. Show completed sessions separately with ratings and retrospective links. Never paste the full table into chat.

Before a lesson, link the backlog and show exactly three strongest topics as `ID — capability → session result`.
